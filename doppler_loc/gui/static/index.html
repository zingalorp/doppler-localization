Recording

?

The audio file, the day it was recorded (UTC) and the area to search. The start time is not needed, the search finds it, but if you know it (a camera's clock, a call log) set it: then every pass you haven't rejected votes for places, a single pass can be enough, and pairs are pinned down much harder.

Click the map to move the area's centre, then save. Or search a shape: pick a country or US state, or press Draw on map and click its corners (double-click to finish). The search leaves out whatever lies outside it, so a smaller shape means fewer wrong places to confuse it with.

Click along the curve, from start to end.
?

Passes are found automatically and sorted by how much they look like a plane the search can explain. Accept the ones to search with, reject the rest, or trace a curve yourself.

Accept only what you're sure is a plane: a wrong pass sends the search to the wrong place, so leave doubtful lines on ?. Keep two or three long, straight passes (the clean S of a plane flying past): the search starts from those. Turning planes are common near airfields; accept them if you're sure, but their speed and distance come out too low, so they can't carry a search on their own. Trace the whole curve, flat ends included, and stay on one curve where two cross. The README's Good traces matter most has more.

Scroll to zoom time, shift+scroll to zoom frequency, drag to pan, click a curve to select it. Keys: ↑/↓ previous/next pass, A accept, U undecided, R reject, H hide or show the curves, hold Space to hide them while held.

Press the speaker, then click the spectrogram to hear the recording from there, with a line following the sound. Press it again to stop.

For any pass, Show harmonics in its details draws its traced line at multiples of its pitch, to check against what is bright there, and the points you place while tracing too. A line with at least two harmonics found is sharpened with them when measured. Lines that are harmonics of another pass aren't listed: its details link to them.

Confidence is how well a straight, steady pass fits the line, so turning planes score low. With the start time known, the search also matches every pass with the flights' real tracks: the Flights column is the share of the line the best flight follows anywhere in the area, turns and all. A line no flight follows is a bird, a vehicle or a plane without ADS-B.

Once there are results, the selected candidate's modelled curves (step 5) are drawn solid in each pass's colour, so you can see which passes it explains.

Search

?

Every pair of accepted passes is searched against the flights; with more than two, pairs are ranked by how much of both traced lines their best flights follow.

With the recording's start time set, every pass you haven't rejected also votes for the places where a flight explains it: real passes agree on one place, false ones (birds, vehicles, planes missing from the flight data) scatter.

Candidates

?

Candidate spots, ranked by how many passes their place and start time explain (the accepted ones, or every voting pass when the start is known) plus, when the start is known, how many flights are heard there on the spectrogram (S1, S2...: their curves gather its peaks, traced or not). The right answer should account for most real passes, not just the ones it was found from.

Pick one to see, for every pass it explains, the flight and its modelled curve on the spectrogram, then mark it right or wrong. Click a selected candidate or flight again (or press Esc) to unselect it; the button under the zoom shows the map full screen.

Conf. is how likely a spot is right, to within about a kilometre, from its lead over the best spot elsewhere and the share of accepted passes it explains.

Click the map to check any spot in the area as a candidate, with its own confidence and curves. Keep it to hold on to it: otherwise the next click replaces it.

Fits? is your check of each curve at this candidate only, and ranks the candidates again at once: Yes counts the pass in full, ? half, No not at all. To stop using a pass everywhere, reject it in step 2, then re-check.
